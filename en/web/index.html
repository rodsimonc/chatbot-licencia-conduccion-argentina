🚦 Argentina Driving License

This is a demo page. The chatbot is in the bottom right corner 👉 click the blue 💬 button and ask it about the ANSV Driver's Manual.

Questions to try:
  • What documentation do I need to drive?
  • What does the yellow traffic light mean?
  • At what age can I get a license?
  • What do I do at a roundabout?
  • What safety elements must the car have?
How to add it to your page

Paste this line before </body> on any site. Replace the URL with your server's when you publish it.

<script src="http://localhost:8000/widget.js"
        data-api="http://localhost:8000" defer></script>

Optional: data-title="Another name" to change the chat title.
